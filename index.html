        Pachinko Guide

        Pachinko in one minute

        Pachinko is a Japanese arcade game — think pinball meets a slot machine. You launch small steel balls into a board full of pins. Most balls bounce down and fall out the bottom. But when a ball drops into the center pocket, the slot reels on the screen spin. Line up three matching numbers and it’s a JACKPOT: the machine pays out lots of balls.

        Shika Story (鹿物語) is deerman_247’s pachinko machine. Everyone in chat shares the same machine on stream and plays it with chat commands or the buttons on the Stream page.

        Balls are free points — no real money, ever.

        How a game goes

        1. Get ballsChat to earn them (10 for your first comment, then 1 per comment), plus a daily login bonus and a few just for being in chat.
        2. FirePress Fire on the Stream page (or type !発射 5 in chat). Softer / Harder change the launch strength.
        3. Hit the center pocketA ball in the center pocket (ヘソ, heso) starts a spin. Up to 4 spins can wait in line (保留, horyū).
        4. Reach!When two reels match, it’s a reach (リーチ). The show that follows hints at how likely the third reel is to match.
        5. JackpotThree of a kind is a 大当たり (ōatari). The balls go to whoever’s ball started that spin, and the last 5 people who hit the pocket in the 3 minutes before get cashback.
        6. RUSHAbout half of the jackpots start RUSH (確変, kakuhen): 4 bonus spins at 1-in-8 odds. Hit again and the RUSH keeps going!

        Words you’ll see on screen

        The machine is in Japanese. Here is what the words mean.

        持ち玉mochidama
        Your balls. 持ち玉ランキング ranks who has the most.
        大当たりōatari
        Jackpot! It pays out over several rounds (ROUND 1/8 …).
        回転数kaitensū
        How many spins the machine has done.
        確率 1/60kakuritsu
        The odds: about 1 jackpot every 60 spins.
        ハマりhamari
        Spins since the last jackpot — a dry streak.
        天井までtenjō made
        Spins left until the “ceiling”. Reach it and 遊タイム starts.
        遊タイムyū-time
        A rescue after a long dry streak: for 20 spins an extra gate opens to let more balls in.
        保留 ・ 抽選まちhoryū
        Spins waiting in line (up to 4), and whose they are.
        リーチrīchi
        A reach: two reels match. SPリーチ and SPSPリーチ are stronger — roughly 5% → 30% → 65% to win.
        確変 ・ RUSHkakuhen
        Boosted odds after a jackpot: 1 in 8 for 4 spins.
        押せ ・ 連打ose ・ renda
        “Push!” / “Mash!” — press the big PUSH button as fast as you can.
        最終決戦saishū kessen
        The final battle on the last RUSH spin — everyone mashes PUSH.
        予告yokoku
        Hint effects before a result. The color tells you how hot it is (below).
        プライズ ・ ジャックポットprize ・ jackpot
        Prize balls float on the board. Knock 10 down and the Jackpot stage starts, paid from the pool of missed balls.

        Special modes

        Sometimes the machine switches into a special mode for a few spins. The board, music and colors change, and payouts swing bigger. When a mode runs out of spins there’s a chance to extend it, and some modes can level up into stronger ones.

        ブチ切れRageThe deer loses it! Happens when PUSH is spammed with no chance going on.
        クラッキングCrackingHacker mode. Can break into Inferno, Golden Deer or Paradox.
        インフェルノInfernoEverything’s on fire. Can heat up into Golden Deer.
        金鹿プレミアムGolden DeerA premium mode with big payout swings. Can upgrade to Platinum.
        プラチナPlatinum+5–25 balls every time you hit the center pocket, with its own roulette and prizes.
        レインボーRainbowThe rarest: +50–100 balls per center pocket. Its Hyper Jackpot gives 2,500 balls to everyone who played along.
        アストロAstroThe board turns into pinball. Every bumper hit pays +1–5 balls.
        パラドックスParadoxTime bends: 30% of misses rewind, and sometimes the machine predicts the future.
        アビスAbyssEvery miss sinks one level deeper, and payouts grow 20% per level (up to ×3).
        ネビュラNebulaBoss battle! Everyone shoots the boss together and splits the reward.

        Rare surprise: 1 in 200 balls in the center pocket spins the red-and-black roulette, which can send you straight into a mode.
